There is a particular freedom in deciding that a thing can stay small.

Small does not mean careless. It means the edges can be understood, the decisions can be revisited, and the details can receive the time they deserve. The best independent software often feels this way: not incomplete, but complete within a carefully chosen boundary.

I am trying to build more things with that kind of boundary.

## Fewer promises, better kept

A narrow tool can make a clear promise. It can open quickly, explain itself without a tour, and become familiar after a few minutes. Every feature has to earn its place because there is nowhere for it to hide.

That constraint is not a lack of ambition. It is the shape that lets the work become real.
